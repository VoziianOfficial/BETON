1. Overview

BETON uses limited cookies and browser storage to support website functionality, remember policy choices, improve user experience, and help maintain a clear concrete provider comparison platform.

BETON is not a direct concrete contractor. The website helps users explore concrete provider options and request quote paths from independent local providers.

Important: The cookie banner stores your Accept or Decline choice so it does not keep appearing after you make a selection.

2. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files that websites may place on your browser or device. They can help websites remember preferences, keep pages working properly, or understand basic usage patterns.

Similar technologies, including localStorage, may store small pieces of information in your browser. This website uses localStorage to remember your cookie consent choice.

3. How We Use Cookies

BETON may use cookies or similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Remembering cookie consent choices
  • Keeping the website functional and responsive
  • Supporting navigation, forms, menus, and accessibility features
  • Understanding general website usage and performance
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing misuse

Cookie usage does not change BETON’s role as an independent comparison platform. Concrete work is performed by independent providers, not by BETON.

4. localStorage

This website uses browser localStorage to save the cookie banner decision. The stored value may indicate whether a user selected “Accept” or “Decline.”

The consent value is stored in the browser under a key used by the website, such as betonCookieConsent. This helps prevent the banner from showing repeatedly after a choice is made.

Clearing your browser storage, using private browsing, or switching devices may reset this preference and cause the banner to appear again.

5. Managing Cookie Choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow users to block, delete, or limit cookies and stored site data.

If you block or delete cookies and localStorage, some website features may not remember preferences, including the cookie banner choice.

  1. Open your browser settings.
  2. Find privacy, security, cookies, or site data settings.
  3. Clear or adjust stored data for this website.
  4. Reload the website to apply the updated preference.

6. Updates to This Policy

BETON may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect website changes, compliance needs, or operational updates.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates the latest version available on this website.
