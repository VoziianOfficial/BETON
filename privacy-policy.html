1. Overview

BETON is an independent concrete service comparison platform. The website helps users explore local provider options for concrete driveways, patios, slabs, foundations, and stamped concrete projects.

This website does not perform concrete work directly. Providers are independent, and users are responsible for reviewing provider qualifications, licenses, insurance, pricing, and project terms before hiring any provider.

Important: BETON is a comparison and quote request platform, not a direct concrete contractor.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily submit through the website, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • ZIP code
  • Concrete project type
  • Project details submitted through forms
  • Consent confirmation related to platform use

We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, cookie preference, and general usage activity to keep the website functional and improve user experience.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to:

  • Respond to concrete provider option requests
  • Help users start a quote request path
  • Organize project information by category and location
  • Provide platform support and communication
  • Improve website performance, usability, and compliance
  • Maintain records related to user requests and policy preferences

BETON does not use submitted information to claim that it performs concrete work directly. Any concrete service work is performed by independent providers.

4. Information Sharing

We may share request information with independent service providers, partner networks, or operational service vendors when needed to help process a user’s concrete provider request.

These third parties may contact users regarding concrete service options, quote paths, or related project information. BETON does not control independent provider pricing, licensing, availability, project terms, or work quality.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect website security, to enforce policies, or to prevent misuse of the platform.

5. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit a form request. You may also use the cookie banner to accept or decline non-essential cookie preferences where applicable.

To request updates, corrections, or deletion of information you submitted through this website, contact us using the information below. We may need to retain certain records when required for legal, operational, or security reasons.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to help protect information submitted through the website. However, no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal, financial, medical, or unrelated private information through the contact form.
